# Weekly Schedule — Disciplined Trader's Life

> **Philosophy:** No wasted time. No mindless scrolling. Every block has purpose.
> Train like the Greeks — progressive intensity through the week, peak on Saturday, full rest on Sunday.
> This is a living document. Adjust times as needed, but protect the structure.

---

## Ground Rules

1. **No mindless scrolling** — phone stays off or in another room during focus blocks
2. **Brush teeth after eating**, not before
3. **Close the GUI window, never the console** (SuperTradeBros habit carries into life discipline)
4. **Coffee/water + snack → Prayer → Brush teeth** — this order every morning, no exceptions
5. **Shower after the main workout only** (no morning shower on weekdays)
6. **Dishes immediately after every meal** — never let them pile up
7. **Screen curfew at 9:00 PM** — only books after this point
8. **8.5 hours of sleep minimum** — sleep is the #1 trading edge. Rested mind = sharp decisions.
9. **Sunday is sacred** — rest, church, meal prep, nothing strenuous

## Training Philosophy — Greek Periodization

Inspired by the ancient Greek tetrad system, adapted to a modern 6-day week:

| Day | Intensity | Location | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | **Light (50%)** | Home | Upper push + pull-ups — wake the body up |
| Tuesday | **Light-Moderate (60%)** | Home | Lower body calisthenics — build the legs |
| Wednesday | **Moderate (70%)** | Commercial Gym | Upper body compound lifts |
| Thursday | **Moderate-Heavy (75%)** | Home | Full body calisthenics + skills |
| Friday | **Heavy (85%)** | Commercial Gym | Lower body + heavy pulls |
| Saturday | **Peak (95%)** | Commercial Gym | Full body power — week's climax |
| Sunday | **Rest (0%)** | — | Complete recovery, light walk only |

## Morning Yoga Flow (15 minutes) — Daily

Do this same flow every morning. It becomes meditative through repetition.

| # | Pose | Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mountain Pose (Tadasana) | 30 sec | Stand tall, ground through feet, set intention |
| 2 | Sun Salutation A | 5 min (3 rounds) | Flow: forward fold → plank → chaturanga → up dog → down dog |
| 3 | Warrior I (each side) | 1 min each | Deep lunge, arms overhead, open chest |
| 4 | Warrior II (each side) | 1 min each | Wide stance, arms extended, gaze over front hand |
| 5 | Triangle Pose (each side) | 30 sec each | Extend and reach, open the side body |
| 6 | Standing Forward Fold | 1 min | Let head hang heavy, release the spine |
| 7 | Cat-Cow | 1 min (10 rounds) | On all fours, sync breath with movement |
| 8 | Child's Pose | 1 min | Knees wide, arms extended, forehead to mat |
| 9 | Seated Twist (each side) | 30 sec each | Gentle spinal rotation |
| 10 | Savasana | 1 min | Flat on back, eyes closed, full release |

### SHOPPING TIPS

1. **Buy proteins in bulk** — portion and freeze what you won't use within 3-4 days
2. **Check pantry first** — cross off spices and oils you already have
3. **Fresh produce timing** — buy Week 2 produce mid-week or get heartier veggies that last
4. **Prep the list** — organize by store section to save time
5. **Stick to the list** — no impulse buys. Discipline starts at the grocery store.
