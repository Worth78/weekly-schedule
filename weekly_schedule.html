Weekly Schedule — Disciplined Trader's Life

Philosophy: No wasted time. No mindless scrolling. Every block has purpose.
Train like the Greeks — progressive intensity through the week, peak on Saturday, full rest on Sunday.
This is a living document. Adjust times as needed, but protect the structure.

Ground Rules

  1. No mindless scrolling — phone stays off or in another room during focus blocks
  2. Brush teeth after eating, not before
  3. Close the GUI window, never the console (SuperTradeBros habit carries into life discipline)
  4. Coffee/water + snack → Prayer → Brush teeth — this order every morning, no exceptions
  5. Shower after the main workout only (no morning shower on weekdays)
  6. Dishes immediately after every meal — never let them pile up
  7. Screen curfew at 9:00 PM — only books after this point
  8. 8.5 hours of sleep minimum — sleep is the #1 trading edge. Rested mind = sharp decisions.
  9. Sunday is sacred — rest, church, meal prep, nothing strenuous

Training Philosophy — Greek Periodization

Inspired by the ancient Greek tetrad system, adapted to a modern 6-day week:

DayIntensityLocationFocus
MondayLight (50%)HomeUpper push + pull-ups — wake the body up
TuesdayLight-Moderate (60%)HomeLower body calisthenics — build the legs
WednesdayModerate (70%)Commercial GymUpper body compound lifts
ThursdayModerate-Heavy (75%)HomeFull body calisthenics + skills
FridayHeavy (85%)Commercial GymLower body + heavy pulls
SaturdayPeak (95%)Commercial GymFull body power — week's climax
SundayRest (0%)—Complete recovery, light walk only

14-Day Meal Plan with Recipes

Principles: High protein, balanced carbs and veggies. Simple prep. Nothing over 30 min cook time.
Portions are for one person. Scale as needed.
Morning snack (with coffee) is always something small: banana, handful of almonds, rice cake, or a few dates.
Pre-workout snack options rotate: apple + PB, banana, trail mix, protein bar, rice cake + honey, Greek yogurt.

DAY 1 — Monday (Week 1)

Breakfast: Scrambled Eggs with Toast and Berries
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Salt, pepper
  • 2 slices whole wheat toast
  • 1/2 cup mixed berries
Whisk eggs with salt and pepper. Melt butter in pan over medium-low. Add eggs, stir gently until just set (2-3 min). Serve with toast and berries on the side. Lunch: Grilled Chicken, Brown Rice, and Broccoli
  • 6 oz chicken breast (from meal prep)
  • 3/4 cup brown rice (from meal prep)
  • 1 cup steamed broccoli (from meal prep)
  • Soy sauce or hot sauce to taste
Reheat chicken, rice, and broccoli. Drizzle with soy sauce. Done in 3 min. Dinner: One-Pan Lemon Herb Salmon
  • 6 oz salmon fillet
  • 1 medium sweet potato, cubed
  • 1 cup asparagus, trimmed
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon (juice + zest)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt, pepper, dried thyme
Preheat oven to 400F. Toss sweet potato cubes with half the olive oil, salt, pepper — roast 15 min. Toss asparagus with remaining oil, place salmon on same pan. Top salmon with garlic, lemon zest, juice, thyme. Roast another 12-15 min. Total: 30 min.

DAY 2 — Tuesday (Week 1)

Quick dinner tonight — algo class at 6 PM.
Breakfast: Oatmeal Power Bowl
  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup water or milk
  • 1 scoop protein powder (vanilla or chocolate)
  • 1 banana, sliced
  • 1 tbsp walnuts
  • Drizzle of honey
Cook oats in water/milk (5 min stovetop or 2 min microwave). Stir in protein powder. Top with banana, walnuts, honey. Lunch: Turkey Avocado Wrap
  • 1 large flour tortilla
  • 5 oz deli turkey (from meal prep batch or deli)
  • 1/2 avocado, sliced
  • Handful mixed greens
  • Tomato slices
  • Mustard or mayo
Lay tortilla flat. Layer turkey, avocado, greens, tomato. Add mustard. Roll tight. Slice in half. 5 min. Dinner: Quick Ground Beef Tacos (Must be done by 5:30)
  • 8 oz lean ground beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning (or: 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp chili powder, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, salt)
  • 4 small corn or flour tortillas
  • Toppings: shredded cheese, salsa, sour cream, shredded lettuce
Brown beef in skillet (5 min). Drain fat. Add seasoning + 1/4 cup water, simmer 3 min. Warm tortillas. Assemble. Total: 12 min.

DAY 3 — Wednesday (Week 1)

Breakfast: Greek Yogurt Parfait
  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1/3 cup granola
  • 1/2 cup blueberries
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
Layer yogurt, granola, blueberries in a bowl. Drizzle honey. Sprinkle chia seeds. No cooking. 2 min. Lunch: Chicken Stir-Fry with Rice
  • 6 oz chicken breast, sliced (from meal prep)
  • 1 cup stir-fry vegetables (bell peppers, snap peas, carrots — from meal prep)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 3/4 cup jasmine rice (from meal prep)
Heat sesame oil in a skillet. Stir-fry chicken and veggies 4-5 min until hot. Add soy sauce. Serve over rice. 8 min. Dinner: Pork Chops with Mashed Potatoes and Green Beans
  • 2 boneless pork chops (~5 oz each)
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika
  • 2 medium russet potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 2 tbsp butter + splash of milk
  • 1 cup green beans
Boil potatoes until tender (15 min). While boiling: season pork chops, pan-sear in oiled skillet 4 min per side. Steam green beans 5 min. Mash potatoes with butter and milk. Total: 25 min.
